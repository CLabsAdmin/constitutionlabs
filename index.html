American Democracy Is in Trouble.
We Can Fix It.

Trust in government has fallen to historic lows. Big money drowns out the voice of average Americans. Hyper-partisan gridlock makes legislative reform nearly impossible.

These aren’t character failures — they’re structural ones. And the Founders gave us exactly the right tool to address them: constitutional amendments.

Over the past few generations, America has forgotten how to use this powerful mechanism to restore government by the people, for the people. This is where that conversation begins again.
