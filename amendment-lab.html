Amendment Lab

Where reform ideas get tested.

A working lab for constitutional reform — we conduct original research, analyze proposed structural amendments, and build tools for the road to ratification.

The work we do in our Lab is focused in three specific areas:

1
Research

To develop better insight and address any gaps in America’s understanding of the use of constitutional amendments, we conduct primary research and publish our findings.

Jump to Research ↓
2
Amendment Analysis

For specific amendment ideas that align with our focus on structural (not policy) reforms, we assemble teams of experts to objectively analyze the prospective benefits and risks of the amendment against its stated objectives. We also assess the prospects for ratification based on the supporting versus opposing forces likely to emerge.

Jump to Amendment Analysis ↓
3
Ratification Roadmap

As a resource for anyone pursuing any type of amendment, we maintain off-the-shelf tools that promote a better understanding of the steps involved in seeking ratification of an amendment.

Jump to the Ratification Roadmap ↓

Amendment Analysis

A standard framework for structural reform.

We have developed a standard framework we apply to assess the strengths and vulnerabilities of a proposed amendment for structural reform. Submitted projects are selected for the Lab by our screening committee and then managed by a designated Lab Leader and a team of volunteers who bring expertise across the spectrum of legal, data analytics, research, public policy, and other skills appropriate to the circumstances. The team applies the framework and provides an analysis to the amendment sponsor(s).
